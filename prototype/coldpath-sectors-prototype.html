● Layout: equal weighting

All industry sectors, equally weighted

This prototype replaces the cold-chain-first / “others” arrangement used in coldpath-prototype.html. Every sector gets the same card, the same three qualification criteria (C1 meter scale · C2 price exposure · C3 shed buffer, per EXPANSION-PLAN.md §0) and the same slot count — order is alphabetical, so no row reads as primary. Build status is honestly tagged LIVE (connector shipped), BUILDING, or PLANNED: equal layout never means fabricated data.

Why parity is defensible: the qualification model is sector-agnostic by construction. A sector earns its place on evidenced C1/C2/C3 facts, and the continuous-process exclusion removes unsuitable loads categorically — at the gate, not by scoring them into an “other” bin.
