03This is not a mockup — the differentiating source is already built and runlive EPA pull, 11 September 2026

The cold-chain unlock. Any US facility holding more than 10,000 lb of anhydrous ammonia must file a Risk Management Plan with EPA — and those filings are public. Ammonia is the dominant industrial refrigerant. The RMP registry is therefore a near-complete, free enumeration of every significant refrigeration site in the United States, with charge size in pounds, location, accident history and a five-year filing cycle that predicts the next capital event.

A purchased firmographic list tells you a company exists. This tells you it runs a large ammonia plant at a specific address — which is the actual qualification criterion. No competitor is using it.

Entity resolution did real work

51 of 114 accounts (45%) were filed under more than one legal name. Americold alone appears as Americold Logistics, LLC (88 sites), Americold (8), Americold Realty (5) and Americold Realty Trust (3).

A naive customer blocklist fails here. Matching only the string "Americold" would have queued an 88-site flagship customer for outbound. This is the worst failure mode available to the system, so it is the first thing acceptance testing targets.

The data is dirty, and the gate caught it

One facility — Neches Terminal, Beaumont TX, a marine terminal — declares 89,000,000 lb of ammonia. That is 445× the p99 and 78% of all ammonia reported in the entire raw pull. Median across 748 sites is 20,098 lb.

Unvalidated it scored 60 and ranked #15 of 111 prospects, above Hormel and Cargill. The validation gate flags it, excludes it from scoring, retains it, and writes it to a human review queue. Nothing is dropped silently.

05Why it will not make things upthe question every buyer asks, answered structurally rather than with a promise

Confidence is enforced, not advisory

3 HighRegulator filing, SEC document, official release. Safe for prospect-facing copy.
2 MedTrade press or professional profile. Internal documents only, flagged.
1 GapPublished as a gap with a resolution path. Never in outbound.

A person cannot exist at confidence ≥2 unless the source's raw payload literally contains that name string. An invented executive is structurally impossible. Titles older than 180 days auto-downgrade.

Generation fails loudly

Each template declares a confidence floor per field. When a required field falls below it, generation refuses and records why — it does not quietly ship a weaker artefact.

This is live in the prototype: NewCold's outbound sequence is blocked because its primary contact is Medium confidence and the account has no CRM owner. The refusal is the system working.

Every rendered deliverable displays its own confidence floor and how many gaps affected it.

The human gate stays

Marketing drafts → reviews → publishes. Reps never see a draft. One person owns voice, positioning and accuracy, so six reps do not each invent their own version of the company story.

Judgement that cannot be automated lives here: that sustainability is the wrong door at Tyson, and that EPA's May 2026 deadline extension killed the compliance pitch at NewCold.

In the prototype, 5 of 17 executive records are published as gaps. Budget ~30 min per account of review, not zero.

The gate to Phase 2 — measured, not asserted

Days 1–10 build it. The measurement happens in the following two weeks, when reps use the briefs on live opportunities. That window needs ~2h/week of marketer time and a 15-minute daily standup — no engineering.

  • 10 briefs published through the review gate
  • ≥8 of 10 rated "usable as-is or with minor edits"
  • ≥3 live opportunities worked from a published brief
  • Median review time ≤30 min per account (45 hard ceiling)
  • Both reps rate it more useful than their current process
  • A written Phase 2 recommendation with measured gap rate

Negative test, pass/fail: Gordon Food Service has 7 sites and zero recorded accidents — almost no signal. Its brief must honestly report that. If the engine invents a buying window there, it fails.
